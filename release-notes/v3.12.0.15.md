## Tales of Milestones

- **Lucis and Resplendent tools in the export.** "Copy collections" and "Copy everything" now include Lucis Tools (Mastercraft, Supra and Lucis) and Resplendent Tools in the relic stages.
- **Doman Enclave in the export.** The export now says when you finished Lighting the Way, the quest that ends Doman Enclave Reconstruction.

## Adventurer's Ledger (Pandora Lunar)

- **Fix: the Relics area counts weapons and tools.** The Relics overview counted relic quests, so a row could show as complete when you held no weapon from that line. The area now has Weapons and Tools tabs, with one panel for each relic line. Each panel shows the number complete, for example "1 of 102 Complete", and opens to a grid of stages and jobs. A held stage also counts the stages before it. The rail meter counts weapons.
- **Relic quests moved.** The relic quest counts are now in Story, under Questlines.
- **Lucis and Resplendent tools.** The Tools tab has panels for these two lines. To fill them, update Tales of Milestones to this version and import a new export. Until you do this, the two panels show 0.
- **Doman Enclave.** After Lighting the Way, the Doman Enclave row shows as optional. The weekly meter and the import's feature count leave it out.
- **Challenges menu.** The site bar has a Challenges menu with Apex Legends and a link to request a game.
- **Fix:** the aether current total no longer counts retired currents. The total is now 303, not 448.
