## Tales of Milestones

- **Hunting Log window (`/hunt`).** A new optional feature. Type `/hunt`, or switch it on in Settings. The window shows every Hunting Log monster that you still need, for all your unlocked classes and your Grand Company at the same time. You do not have to change class to see a class's log. Each row shows the class, the task, the monster, your kills and where to find it. Finished monsters, finished ranks and locked classes are not shown.
- **Map flags for Hunting Log monsters.** Click Flag on a row to open the map at that monster. If the monster is also in your current zone, the row shows that location. The locations come from the Hunty plugin by Infiziert90 (MIT License).
- **Current class only and Current zone only.** Two check boxes in the Hunting Log window. With Current zone only, the window shows only the monsters in the zone where you are, for all classes. The window updates when you change class or zone.
- **Change class from the Hunting Log.** In the summary line at the top of the window, click a class name to equip a gear set for that class. If you have more than one gear set for the class, a list opens and you select one. The game's own Gear Set function does the change, the same as a click in the Gear Set window. Nothing is sent to chat.
- **Hunting Log in the export.** The export has a new `huntLog` block with each class's rank and kill counts. The Adventurer's Ledger reads it.
- **Leve markers.** In the quest list, a leve whose items are in your bags now shows "[R]" in place of a check mark. The check mark means "complete" in the News panel. Hold the pointer on a leve row to see what "> " and "[R]" mean.
- **Fix: allied societies in the mini window.** The society list shows only societies that are capped and wait for a main quest. Societies at their last rank are not listed.

## Adventurer's Ledger (Pandora Lunar)

- **Hunting Log.** Jobs has a Hunting Log frame. It shows each class's rank, its tasks complete, and the monsters it still needs, with the place. Import a Tales of Milestones export to fill it in.
- **Gear guide.** Jobs has a Gear guide frame. Select one job and level. It shows the highest item level for each slot from the sources that you tick, with options for your export, crafting, NPC vendors and the market board.
- **Mentor Roulette readiness.** The Mentor tab has a Roulette frame, also listed under Jobs and Duties. It shows the Battle Mentor requirements: the level 100 role quests, a combat job at level 100, and each duty in the game's Mentor Roulette pool cleared once. Missing duties are listed by expansion and type. Item level 755 is a manual check, because the export does not have it.
- **Import preview: Challenge Log.** When an export opens new Challenge Log entries, the import preview shows them, for example "Challenge Log entries open: 40 → 41 (new: Soul Survivor I)".
- **Allied societies.** When every society in a group is at its last rank and the Intersocietal Quests are done, the group shows only the names, ranks and the quest check box.
- **Fix: Ocean Fishing clock.** The bar shows the time as "29:58" again, not "00:29:58".
- **Fix: new characters.** A new character no longer stops the Quest items frame after you enter a crafter or gatherer level. "Mark items HQ" is on by default again.
- **Fix: backup import.** A backup with no characters no longer leaves the page empty until a reload.
- **Fix: Challenge Log.** The Challenge Log list now comes from the game's data, so it matches the game after a patch.
- **Fix: smaller items.** The Collections filter (All, Owned, Missing) keeps its selection when you change area. Collapsed sections stay collapsed. Ended events leave the events feed. The time zone and hunt switches work with Space and Enter.
- **Faster pages.** The Ledger draws only the area and frame on screen.
