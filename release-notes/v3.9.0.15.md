## Time Memoria

- **Relic stages in the collections export.** "Copy collections" and "Copy everything" now include the highest relic stage that you hold for each job, from Zodiac to Cosmic Tools. Time Memoria reads the armoury chest, the bags, the saddlebag, your retainers and the glamour dresser. You do not have to open your retainers: the game's own Search for Item data is used.
- **Keybound Brawler name.** Active Events shows the Gold Saucer event (festival 257) by name, not as "Unknown". The event starts on 28 October 2026 at 08:00 UTC.

## Adventurer's Ledger (Pandora Lunar)

- **Relics area.** Relics is now its own area in the left rail, with an overview and one tab for each expansion. Each tab shows the stage of each job's relic weapon or tool, the relic quests done, and the full list of stages.
- **Rail meters.** Daily, Weekly and Seasonal have their own rows and meters in the rail, and each tab shows a percentage. The 30-day window for routines counts game days, from the daily reset.
- **Events.** The Events tab and the rail's event slides read from the Time Memoria news feed. The slides change while the page is open.
- **Collections.** Fish and spearfish lists come from the game's own tables.
- **Jobs.** The crafter and gatherer hub quests from level 70 to 100 are listed.
- **Import.** The import summary names the Master's Bestiary and folklore tome rows. The page reloads when the import dialog closes after a merge.
- **Route line.** The route line says why it is empty when it has no data.
- **Fix:** a second open Ledger tab no longer erases changes that you made in the first tab.
