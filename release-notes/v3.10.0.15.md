## Time Memoria

- **New /tm food window.** For each role (DPS, Healer, Tank, Crafting and Gathering), the window shows the strongest meal that you hold and where it is: your inventory, your saddlebag or a retainer by name. The top line shows the food that you ate and the time that remains. If you hold no meal for a role, the window tells you to use any food for the EXP bonus.
- **Where each food is.** In the Bonuses tab, put the cursor on the name of a food. A tooltip shows each place that holds it, in NQ and HQ: inventory, saddlebag, premium saddlebag and each retainer.
- **Saddlebag food after a restart.** The Bonuses tab and /tmmini find food in your saddlebag immediately after you start the game. Before, they found it only after you opened the saddlebag. The premium saddlebag is now included.
- **Report an issue from the game window.** The Help tab of the game-styled window has an "Open the issue tracker" button. Both Help tabs tell you that you must have a GitHub account to open an issue.
- **Saved exports.** Time Memoria keeps your last 5 exports for each character in the exports folder of its configuration folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Fix:** the Ledger export no longer counts the Hall of the Novice exercises as duties. One export showed 202 duties when the correct count was 192.
