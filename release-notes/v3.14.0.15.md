## Tales of Milestones

- **Hunt group counts.** The export now carries the game's count for each of the 16 A and S rank "Mark of the ..." achievements (A Realm Reborn, Heavensward and Stormblood). A finished group is read when the Achievements window loads. The count is for the group only. The plugin does not record which mark you killed.
- **Read hunt counts.** A new button on the Progression tab, in both windows, asks the game for all 16 counts, one at a time (about 10 seconds). It runs only when you press it. It is the only request that the plugin sends to the game server.
- **/tmwiki: allied society quests and levequests.** Allied society quests are in their own group under each expansion, with one heading for each society. Held levequests are listed in their own section, grouped by town. Each one opens its wiki page.
- **Food location on the Bonuses tab.** Each food row shows where the food is: B1 to B4 for the inventory block (the same number as /isearch), and S for the saddlebag.
- **Journal re-baseline in the native window.** The Settings tab of the native window can now re-baseline the journal, as the classic window can. Click Set Baseline, then click again within 5 seconds to confirm.
- **Lodestone links in the native window.** Maintenance and Active Events rows have a Read button after the title. It opens the Lodestone page.
- **Ko-fi heart.** The classic main window has a heart in its title bar. A click opens the Ko-fi page. It is a link only.
- **Main Scenario pacing.** The Main Scenario rate stops at the final Main Scenario quest, so play after it does not change the rate. The classic window now shows the Main Scenario row too.
- **Adventurer's Ledger link.** "Open Adventurer's Ledger" now opens legendmemoria.org/lunar.
- **Keybound Brawler end date.** The news feed now gives Keybound Brawler's end, the release of Patch 8.0, so Active Events shows when it ends.
- **Fix:** the FINAL FANTASY XVI crossover, The Path Infernal, showed twice in Active Events, once as "Festival #147". It now shows once, with its name.
- The AI declaration is corrected and complete, and "tracker" is removed from the plugin's tags.

## Adventurer's Ledger (Pandora Lunar)

- **Hunt counts on the Hunts tab.** After an import from this version, each A and S rank group shows the game's count beside your ticks, for example "The game counts 5 of 6; you have ticked 4". It never ticks anything.
- **Armor cards.** The Armor frame shows one card for each armor set, with "3 of 5 slots" for each role or job that holds a piece. The full grid is inside each card.
- **GARO weapon card.** The GARO frame shows the GARO weapons as a line card, like Weapons and Tools. The GARO armor grid is below it.
- **Where a relic line starts.** When the starting quest of a relic line is not open yet, the card names the first quest to do on the way to it.
- **Custom Deliveries boxes.** The Custom Deliveries routine has 12 boxes, one for each weekly delivery. Each box also counts for The Customer Is Always Right in the Challenge Log.
- **Weekly meter.** The Weekly meter now counts Challenge Log entries that you completed in the last 4 weeks, beside your weekly routines.
- **Crafting solver.** The Jobs area has a crafting solver that finds a rotation for one recipe. It is built from Raphael (Apache-2.0) and runs in your browser.
- **Moved notice.** The old github.io address shows a notice that the Ledger has moved, with a Download backup button.
- **Fix:** the Seasonal meter read the FINAL FANTASY XVI crossover as the FINAL FANTASY XV one and counted the wrong quests.
